# Google Business Profile (GBP) Master Setup Checklist
## Elite Fire and Protection — Faridabad & Delhi NCR

This checklist is prepared specifically for the business owner to claim, verify, and optimize their Google Business Profile adhering to Google's current local representation guidelines.

---

### Step 1: Profile Creation & Business Naming
- [ ] Visit [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business/) and log in with the primary official business Google Account.
- [ ] Enter the exact business name: **Elite Fire and Protection**
  - *Warning:* Do not add extra promotional keywords (e.g., do not name it "Elite Fire and Protection - Best Extinguisher Refilling Shop in Faridabad"). Google suspends profiles for keyword stuffing.

### Step 2: Primary and Secondary Categories
- [ ] **Primary Category:** `Fire protection service`
- [ ] **Secondary Categories (Select only those that apply):**
  - `Fire protection equipment supplier`
  - `Fire protection system supplier`
  - `Security system installation service`

### Step 3: Location vs. Service-Area Business (SAB)
- [ ] If clients visit an official walk-in retail showroom or office with prominent exterior signage, enter the physical street address in Faridabad.
- [ ] If technicians travel directly to client job sites and there is no walk-in customer reception, configure the profile as a **Service-Area Business (SAB)**:
  - Check: "I deliver goods and services to my customers".
  - Add Service Areas: `Faridabad, Haryana`, `Gurugram, Haryana`, `New Delhi, Delhi`, `Noida, Uttar Pradesh`.

### Step 4: Contact & Website Linking
- [ ] **Direct Phone Number:** Add the official business phone number: `+91 88261 11491`.
- [ ] **Website URL:** Enter `https://elitefireandprotection.in/` (use the canonical HTTPS URL).
- [ ] **Appointment Link:** Set to `https://elitefireandprotection.in/contact`.

### Step 5: Business Hours
- [ ] Set Regular Hours:
  - Monday – Saturday: `9:00 AM – 7:00 PM`
  - Sunday: `Closed` or `By Appointment` (for emergency AMC callouts).

### Step 6: Professional Photography & Visual Proof
Google rewards profiles that upload authentic, high-resolution original photographs:
- [ ] Company service vehicle or equipment van.
- [ ] Technicians in branded safety vests, helmets, and safety shoes.
- [ ] In-progress hydrostatic pressure testing of fire extinguishers with pressure gauges visible.
- [ ] Fire pump room piping, manifolds, and jockey pump setups.
- [ ] Close-up of certified IS 2190 inspection tags.
- [ ] *Caution:* Never upload generic stock photos or internet downloads. Google's Vision AI flags generic images and lowers profile authority.

### Step 7: Review Solicitation Workflow
- [ ] Generate your Google Review short-link from the GBP dashboard (`https://g.page/r/.../review`).
- [ ] Create a standard WhatsApp message sent to facility managers immediately after completing an extinguisher refilling delivery or quarterly AMC audit:
  > *"Dear [Client Name], thank you for trusting Elite Fire and Protection with your facility's fire safety in [Faridabad/NCR]. Could you take 30 seconds to share your feedback on our service? Your honest review helps us maintain our quality standards: [Insert Review Shortlink]"*
- [ ] Respond to all reviews professionally within 48 hours.
